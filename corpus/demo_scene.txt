EP07 — "OUTLIERS"
SCENE 12 — INT. ST. ANNE'S ER, TRAUMA BAY 4 — NIGHT

MAYA CHEN, 34, T1DM, LVEF 30%, is wheeled in. Skin pale, diaphoretic.
Monitor shows narrow-complex tachycardia at 182. BP 88/54. SpO2 91% on 4L NC.

    DR. ELENA VASQUEZ (Attending)
        Stable narrow-complex tach, hypotensive.
        Push one of epi, IV. Now.

    DR. RAJ PATEL (Senior Resident)
        Epi's up.

Maya's rhythm degenerates. V-fib on the monitor.

    ELENA
        Charging to 360. Clear!

She defibrillates. Sinus rhythm returns. ETCO2 climbs to 38.

    ELENA
        We've got her. Extubate her.

Raj pulls the tube three minutes post-ROSC. Maya immediately grimaces,
gags, then stabilizes. Elena exhales.

    ELENA (CONT'D)
        Good save, team.
